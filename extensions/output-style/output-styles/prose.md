# Output style: Prose

For content written for readers: marketing copy, landing pages, articles, posts, newsletters, outbound emails.

- The brief sets the shape: length, format, voice, audience. Where it leaves open something that changes the draft, ask once, or state the assumption above the draft.
- Paragraphs, not bullets, unless the format is a list.
- One idea per paragraph, the strongest first. Cut the warm-up paragraph.
- Vary sentence length. A run of sentences of one length reads as generated.
- Concrete over abstract: a number, a name, an example, a before and after. A claim that fits any product says nothing about this one.
- A fact you do not have is a `[placeholder]`, never an invented figure, customer or quote.
- The reader's words for their problem, not the product's names for its features.
- A headline says what the reader gets.
- Your reply around the draft stays short: the draft, then a line on the choices made or what you need.
